This project is a videogame called "mazle".

The rules are simple, there is a grid based maze where the player can only moves onto tiles where the sum of the x and y coordinates equal an even number, and x and y are both odd. Tiles that are even x and y are always impassable corners. Tiles where the sum of the x and y coordinates are odd are either empty, act as walls, or act as open or closed doors, or that tile is the 'exit' (the goal of the maze). Doors are color coded, and passing through an open door switched the open/closed state of all other doors of that color.
